Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Summary

Dubbing AI does not collect your voice, does not upload audio, and does not require an account. All voice processing happens locally on your PC.

What data we collect

We collect nothing from the desktop application. The app runs fully offline and has no telemetry, no analytics, and no network connections.

From this website (dubbingai.one) we collect:

  • Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, page visited) — kept for 30 days for security purposes only
  • No cookies beyond what your browser sets automatically
  • No advertising trackers
  • No third-party analytics

Your voice data

Your microphone audio is processed entirely on your device by the Dubbing AI application. It is never transmitted to our servers or any third party. It is not stored, recorded, or logged anywhere.

No account required

Dubbing AI does not ask for your name, email, age, or any personal information. There is no registration process.

Children's privacy

Dubbing AI does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Since we collect no personal data at all, this is satisfied by design.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. The policy will remain hosted at dubbingai.one/privacy.
