How to add a voice changer to OBS

Run dubbingaiSetup.exe. Adds dubbingai Virtual Mic to Windows.

2
Pick a voice in Dubbing AI

Choose your AI character. Adjust pitch, echo, and tone with the knobs.

3
Set it in OBS

OBS → Settings → Audio → Mic/Auxiliary Audio → dubbingai Virtual Mic

Since Dubbing AI routes audio through a virtual mic, it works with OBS, Streamlabs, StreamElements, TikTok LIVE Studio, Zoom, and any platform that accepts a microphone.
