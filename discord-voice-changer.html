Why use Dubbing AI on Discord?

Unlike browser extensions or online voice changers, Dubbing AI runs entirely on your PC with no cloud uploads. Your voice stays private, the latency is ~12ms, and it works even when your internet goes down.

Frequently asked questions

Does Discord allow voice changers?

Yes. Discord sees Dubbing AI as a regular microphone. Fully compliant with Discord ToS.

Will my friends notice the delay?

No. At 128-sample buffer the added latency is ~12ms — well below what anyone can hear.

Is it free to use on Discord?

Yes. All 8 voice presets are completely free. No subscription, no account.

Can I switch voices mid-conversation?

Yes. Press Ctrl+1 through Ctrl+8 to switch voices instantly without leaving Discord.
