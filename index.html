    Is dubbingai free?

    Yes. Every character in the library is free. No trial timer and no account.

    Can it get me banned in Fortnite?

    dubbingai never reads or changes game files or memory. Windows sees it as a regular microphone, the same as plugging in a USB mic, so the game only receives audio.

    Does my voice get uploaded anywhere?

    No. All processing happens on your PC, and it keeps working with Wi-Fi off.

    Will my friends hear a delay?

    At the default 128-sample buffer dubbingai adds about 12 ms, below what people notice in voice chat.

    Which games and apps work?

    Anything that lets you pick a microphone: Fortnite, Discord, Valorant, CS2, Roblox, OBS, TikTok LIVE Studio, Zoom and more.
